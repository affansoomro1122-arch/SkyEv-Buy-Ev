Exclusive refrigeration

BlackShields electric refrigeration

SKYEV has teamed with DropTruck to offer the BlackShields chassis-mounted electric refrigeration system. This exclusive solution is mounted beneath the body rather than above the cab.

  • Helps preserve vehicle height
  • Reduces weight
  • Eliminates the need for heavy, expensive auxiliary batteries
  • Easy servicing access
  • Low center of gravity
